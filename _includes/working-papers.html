    Tushar Veer Singh

  2. Macro-Financial Linkages and Business Cycle Transmission Between the United Kingdom and Australia
    Tushar Veer Singh
    SSRN Working Paper, October 2026
    SSRN

    Abstract: Using a multi-variable VAR model on a quarterly dataset spanning four decades, this paper models the transmission of shocks through expository financial and trade channels. Preliminary findings reveal an asymmetric relationship in business cycle transmission: an aggregate output shock originating in the UK Granger causes Australia's real GDP, showing a strong one-quarter lag and a weaker two-quarter lag. But no reciprocal effect from Australia to the UK is observed. Beyond the empirical findings, this paper also establishes a theoretical framework linking continuous-time macroeconomic systems to discrete-time VAR representations, providing a robust mathematical foundation for analyzing standard discrete datasets.

  3. Size-Based Policies and Firm Growth: Evidence from Pakistan
    (with Zehra Farooq, Usama Jamal, and Mazhar Waseem)
    Working paper, October 2024

    Abstract: Size-based regulations and taxation are ubiquitous. In this paper, we examine the impact of size-based tax and regulatory thresholds on firm growth in Pakistan.
